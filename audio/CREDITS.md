# Audio credits and licence

All music and sound effects in this folder were composed and synthesised from scratch for Wordle Battle
(oscillators, FM synthesis, filtered noise and a synthetic reverb; no samples, no recordings, no third-party audio).

- They contain no copyrighted material and need no attribution.
- They are original works of this project; use them in Wordle Battle freely, with no royalties.

Layout
- `music/`  home.mp3 (menus), solo.mp3 (Solo + Daily), battle.mp3 (AI Zone, Friend Zone, Team Zone, Series): stereo, 96 kbps, seamless loops.
- `sfx/`    62 short mono effects. `js/audio.js` lists every name and decides when each one plays.

To use a different sound, replace the .mp3 with another file of the same name (keep it short, and keep the loops exactly as long as they are).
