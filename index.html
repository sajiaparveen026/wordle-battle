    How to Play

    • Guess the hidden 5-letter word in 6 attempts.
    • Type a word, then tap Submit. It must be a real word. Invalid words don't use an attempt.
    • After each guess, the tiles change colour to show how close you were:

    ✓Green: W is in the word and in the right spot.

    •Yellow: I is in the word but in the wrong spot.

    Grey: U is not in the word at all.

    Stuck? Use the magnifier to reveal a letter, the crossed circle to remove wrong letters, the lightbulb for a clue about the word, or skip to a new word.

    Guess the hidden word

    Find the secret 5-letter word in 6 attempts.

    Type a real word and press Enter. After every guess, the tiles change colour to show how close you were.

    Read the colours

    ✓Green: W is in the word and in the right spot.

    •Yellow: I is in the word but in the wrong spot.

    Grey: U is not in the word at all.

    Repeated letters are only marked as many times as they appear in the word.

    Example: the secret word is CRANE. Guess TRAIN: R and A are green, N is yellow, T and I are grey. Guess CRANE: every letter is green, so you win in two tries.
