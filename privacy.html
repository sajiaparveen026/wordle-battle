Privacy Policy

Starter text for Wordle Battle. Before a public launch, replace the contact placeholder and have it reviewed for your region.

What we collect

  • If you create an account: your email address, username, and avatar. Your password is handled by our login provider (Supabase) and is never visible to or stored by the game itself.
  • If you use Google sign-in: the email address and name Google shares with us.
  • Your game progress: coins, hints, XP, level and statistics.

Where it is stored

Guest progress stays in your own browser. Account data is stored with Supabase (our database and login provider) so you can sign in on other devices. The installed app also keeps its own files on your device so it works offline.

How it is used

Only to run the game: signing you in, saving progress, and showing your name in the game. We do not sell your data or show ads.

Your choices

You can play as a guest without giving any personal data. To ask for your account and data to be deleted, contact us below.
